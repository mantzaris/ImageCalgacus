## Official ICAART sources

- [Templates](https://icaart.scitevents.org/Templates.aspx) links the authentic SCITEPRESS archive. Its current archive includes a June 2026 Example.tex and the May 2023 style. Required article class and bibliography files are byte-unchanged. `template/provenance.json` records URL, retrieval date and hashes.
- [Guidelines](https://icaart.scitevents.org/Guidelines.aspx) confirm anonymous Regular Paper review, 10,000–50,000 non-whitespace characters including references/tables/graphs/appendices, and a 12-page published Full Paper limit. The latter is not substituted for the character rule.
- [AI Tools](https://icaart.scitevents.org/AiTools.aspx), [FAQ](https://icaart.scitevents.org/FAQ.aspx), and [2027 Call for Papers](https://icaart.scitevents.org/CallForPapers.aspx?y=2027) were inspected for disclosure, anonymity, supplementary status and Area 2 fit.
- The template specifies a 70–200-word abstract, 10-point body, 9-point references/tables/captions, unmodified supplied style files and no running heads or page numbers. No layout parameters were tuned to force pagination.
- No separate Regular Paper supplementary-upload permission was verified. The AI acknowledgement/anonymity conflict and the public-posting restriction are recorded in `AUTHOR_REVIEW.md`.

No reference title, author, venue or DOI is inferred from the numerical results. The literature check is focused, not a claim of exhaustive coverage. Official source pages and template requirements can change before the actual submission date.
