\section{\uppercase{Discussion and Limitations}}
\label{sec:discussion}
Artifact reconstruction, finite capacity and completion jointly govern authenticated recovery. Rank coding provides text capacity with strong known-model detectability. A1 succeeds for generated PNGs but fails on low-information text trajectories. The photograph profile provides exact transport with constrained changes, without improving upon the parity baseline.\aifootnote

Development isolates saved-text serialization. Twenty image payloads under two prompts give 40 packet-paired cases. Complete-prefix filtering recovers 40/40, versus 22/40 for singleton filtering (8/20 forest, 14/20 soup). All 18 drift failures remain unrepaired. This supports the consistency requirement alongside published stepwise verification, not 40 independent payload observations. Twenty historical PNG re-saves also recover exactly. JPEG, cropping and paraphrasing remain untested.

The generated-carrier study has 20 held-out groups per direction and two pinned models. Shared controls and rescoring retain their dependencies. Near-chance AUC or a withheld row does not establish secrecy or resistance to arbitrary observers. A1's capacity and stagnation limitations concern this adaptation, not every arithmetic coder. Photograph fidelity likewise does not establish undetectability.

Graph replay reduces image-model launch overhead. Text filtering still consumes approximately 71\%, 75\% and 89\% of fixed, gated and A1 encoding time. Faster inference cannot remove that CPU bottleneck or supply missing information under the frozen cap. The 5.13-fold benchmark improvement covers three development payloads on the pinned stack, not other hardware or the new tile workload.

The image port has a sale-restricting notice, and checkpoint redistribution permission was not established. BSDS photograph publication rights also require author review. No weights, keys or private row bytes accompany the manuscript.
