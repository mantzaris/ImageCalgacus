## Contribution and novelty assessment

**Contribution statement.** We develop a shared authenticated protocol for exact bidirectional transport between canonical images and saved generated text, and between literal text and saved generated PNGs. A controlled three-coder comparison connects artifact reconstruction, finite capacity and known-model detectability. Paired PNG rescoring establishes the effect of one specified context mismatch, and exact execution comparisons establish a behavior-preserving GPU speedup.

Official rules prohibit publicly posting the submitted paper while under review. Do not push these draft PDFs/source files or place them on a preprint server during that period. Review the already-public project's relationship to the submission with the venue if needed; no existing evidence was hidden or rewritten.
