DISCLOSING AI USE IN MANUSCRIPTS
BOOKS AND PROCEEDINGS
                           As an author, you are required to transparently disclose relevant uses of AI in
                           accordance with Springer Nature’s Editorial AI Policies. This document
                           provides wording for disclosing the use of AI to prepare and write your
                           manuscript. If you used AI during your research or writing process in other
                           ways, then described in this document, please add a transparent disclosure
                           at the end of the chapter.

In any case, you must obtain all necessary rights to generate the content by means of AI and to use
it in the context of the submitted manuscript, ensuring no third-party rights (including intellectual
property rights, personality rights, and similar rights) are infringed, and that any use of AI complies
with applicable laws and regulations.

Our AI policies can be found on the Springer Nature Books Publishing Policy webpage.



TEXT
Use case: The author(s) translated / edited / adapted / corrected / formatted / etc. the text (or
parts thereof) with the assistance of AI in accordance with Springer Nature's Editorial AI Policies,
and reviewed and revised the output. The author(s) take(s) accountability for the final submitted
version, ensuring rights of third-party content have been acquired (if applicable).

Requires a footnote on the first page of the chapter. Recommended text:
This chapter was prepared with the assistance of AI. See disclosure at the end of chapter for
further details.



Also requires full disclosure at the end of the chapter. Recommended text:
[Section X / The manuscript], or parts thereof, was [translated / edited / adapted / corrected /
formatted / etc.] with the assistance of artificial intelligence (AI) using [tool name] developed
by [organization] ([version or model number], [month accessed] [year accessed]). The
generated content was carefully reviewed, verified, and endorsed by the author[s] to ensure
the accuracy and integrity of the final manuscript.
