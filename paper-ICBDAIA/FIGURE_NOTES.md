# Figure provenance and presentation

The three manuscript figures are new layouts of **saved numerical results and actual saved carrier pixels/bytes**. No generation, inference, detector fitting, resampling, timing, or other experiment was performed. The original manuscript, figures, and raw evidence are unchanged.

Run `python3 build_figures.py` from this directory to regenerate the PDFs, SVGs and PNG previews. The script needs Python 3, Matplotlib, NumPy, and Pillow. All inputs needed for these three presentation figures are bundled under `data/`; the manuscript build itself uses the existing PDFs and does not need Python packages. Original source paths and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `data/figure_data.json`; output hashes and package versions are in `data/figure_rendering.json`. The numerical input is a selection of unchanged fields from the authoritative saved analyses, not recomputed uncertainty.

All text is at least 8 pt when the figures are included at 122 mm width. PDF labels and statistical marks are vector graphics. The small image arrays are embedded at their original pixel values and displayed by nearest-neighbor interpolation. No smoothing, replacement examples, or cosmetic editing of carriers was performed. Source image recovery equality and literal-message equality were checked from saved bytes; this is not a new decoder run.

The original `ICAART2027_submission.pdf` was inspected as a 12-page rendering, and the four requested original figures (`figure1_transport_submission`, `cover_transport_submission`, `figure2_recovery_rate`, `context_auc`) were rendered and visually inspected individually. Their broad two-column figure widths would produce small labels at the new text width. The new layout therefore uses a shorter literal excerpt, combines recovery counts with useful-rate markers, and retains the context paired-change comparison. The photograph extension is presented numerically in the new paper; no BSDS photograph or derivative is reproduced in its figures.

## Asset permissions and author decisions

The source image in the overview is derived from Fashion-MNIST (Xiao, Rasul and Vollgraf, 2017), original frozen Zalando revision `b2617bb6d3ffa2e429640350f613e3291e10b141`. Its preserved MIT notice is in `ASSET_NOTICES.md`.

The 63-byte literal text is from Jane Austen's *Pride and Prejudice*, Project Gutenberg ebook 1342, frozen UTF-8 source. It is unrestricted under US copyright; Gutenberg terms and other jurisdictions still apply. The same message is shown twice to make exact recovery concrete.

The original photograph figure was inspected but not reused. BSDS500 test photographs 2018, 3063 and 5096 and their stego/difference derivatives still have unresolved specific republication rights, as described in `../paper/icaart2027/ASSET_NOTICES.md` and `AUTHOR_REVIEW.md`. Research access and dataset citation do not establish publication permission. No rights clearance is claimed. This decision does not change the scientific photograph results or clear the existing public manuscript's asset questions. If photographs are added later, authors must resolve those questions; ×32 pixel differences must remain explicitly labeled analytical visualizations.
