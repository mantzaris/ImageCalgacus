The liquid infusion is absorbed quickly by the vegetables, and the cook stirs the mixture with a wooden spoon, releasing the steam and allowing the flavors to meld. As the soup simmers on the stovetop, the cook attends to the dinner table, setting the plates and glasses with a flourish. Meanwhile, the aroma of the vegetables continues to tantalize the senses, tempting everyone in the household to take a spoon and taste. Finally, after a half-hour of gentle cooking, the soup is ready. A taste reveals a rich, satisfying broth filled with tender, flavorful vegetables. The home cook smiles, knowing that this is indeed a soup that will warm the hearts and bellies of her loved ones.
