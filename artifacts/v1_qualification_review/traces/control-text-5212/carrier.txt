 As she enters the kitchen, Julia decides to prepare a simple vegetable soup for lunch. She starts by washing her hands under the sink and then moving on to the stove. She grabs a large pot and fills it with water, careful not to overflow it. Next, she adds a handful of salt and some recently washed vegetables, including carrots, potatoes, and green beans. Julia knows that the vegetables will release their flavors and nutrients into the water as they simmer. She turns the stove to medium heat and brings the water to a boil, watching carefully as the pot starts to foam and bubble. Once the water has reached boiling temperature, Julia reduces the heat to a gentle simmer and lets the soup cook for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent any vegetables from sticking to the bottom of the pot. As the soup nears the end of its cooking time, Julia adds some dried thyme and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice to give the soup a bit of flavor and freshness. She then turns off the heat and adjusts the seasoning to taste, adding a little more salt if necessary. Finally, she ladles the soup into a bowl and serves it hot, garnished with a sprinkle of chopped fresh parsley. Julia enjoys her delicious, comforting vegetable soup, feeling satisfied and content.
