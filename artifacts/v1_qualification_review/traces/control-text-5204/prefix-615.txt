 Here is a step-by-step guide to making a simple vegetable soup from scratch.
To start, gather all the necessary ingredients and equipment. You'll need a large pot, some vegetable oil, an onion, carrots, celery, potatoes, vegetable broth, and your choice of seasonings. You can use canned broth or make your own by simmering vegetables and bones in water. If you're making your own broth, start by sautéing the vegetable scraps in oil until they're softened, then add water and bring to a boil. Let it simmer for at least an hour to extract all the flavors. Once you have your broth, it's ready to use in the soup.

Next, chop the onion, carrots, and celery into small pieces. These will form the base of your soup. In a large pot, heat about 2 tablespoons of oil over medium heat. Add the chopped vegetables and sauté until they're softened and lightly browned. You can add a little salt and pepper to taste.

Now it's time to add the potatoes. You can peel and chop them into small cubes or leave the skins on and chop them into large chunks. Add the potatoes to the pot and stir to combine.

Pour in the vegetable broth, and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat to low and let it simmer for about 20-30 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender. You can check for doneness by inserting a fork into a potato cube. If it slides in easily, the potato is cooked.

Once the potatoes are tender, you can season the soup with your choice of seasonings. You can use dried herbs like thyme and rosemary, or add a splash of vinegar or lemon juice. Taste and adjust as needed.

Finally, serve the soup hot, garnished with chopped fresh herbs or a dollop of sour cream if desired. You can also cook some crusty bread to mop up any remaining broth.

That's it! With these simple steps, you can make a delicious and comforting vegetable soup from scratch. Just remember to use your own judgment and adjust the recipe to your taste. Happy cooking!
