Once all the vegetables are chopped, Sarah pours a couple of tablespoons of olive oil into the large pot she's using, and swirls it around to coat the bottom evenly. Then, she adds the chopped onion, mixed vegetables, and minced garlic to the pot, stirring them in gently with a wooden spoon to combine the ingredients. Before heating the pot, Sarah adds a large can of vegetable broth and a can of diced tomatoes to the mix, stirring them in well to distribute the moisture evenly. Once the ingredients are combined, Sarah places the pot over the stovetop, turning the heat to medium and allowing the contents of the pot to simmer slowly for about 20 minutes. During this time, she occasionally stirs the pot with the wooden spoon to prevent the ingredients from sticking to the bottom. After the allotted time has passed, Sarah removes the pot from the heat, and lets the soup cool slightly before serving it to her family. Some Tips for Preparing Delicious Vegetable Soup: 
1. **Choose a variety of vegetables**: Select a mix of vegetables that provide a range of flavors, textures, and colors to make your soup more interesting.
2. **Use a flavorful broth**: A good broth is essential to a delicious soup. You can use store-bought broth or make your own by simmering vegetables and herbs in water.
3. **Don't overcook the vegetables**: Cooking the vegetables until they are tender is important, but overcooking can make them mushy and unappetizing.
4. **Add aromatics for flavor**: Onions, garlic, and other aromatics can add a lot of flavor to your soup. Saute them in olive oil before adding the other vegetables.
