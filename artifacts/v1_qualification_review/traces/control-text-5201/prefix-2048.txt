 As I step into the kitchen, I decide to whip up a simple vegetable soup for lunch. I begin by gathering the essential ingredients: onions, carrots, potatoes, and some frozen corn kernels. I peel the onions and chop them into small pieces, taking care not to cut my fingers. Next, I chop the carrots and potatoes into similar-sized pieces, making sure they're all roughly the same size so they cook evenly. Once I've got all the vegetables prepared, I heat up some olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. I add the chopped onions first, letting them sauté for a few minutes until they start to soften and caramelize. Then, I add the chopped carrots and potatoes, stirring well to combine them with the onions. Next, I add a pinch of salt and a few grinds of pepper to taste, followed by a cup of chicken broth and some frozen corn kernels. I stir the mixture well, making sure everything is coated in the flavorful liquid. With the vegetables and broth combined, I bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low and let it cook for about 20 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender. As the soup cooks, I can smell the aroma of the sautéed onions and the tender vegetables mingling with the rich chicken broth. Once the vegetables are cooked to my liking, I give the soup a quick stir and serve it hot, garnished with a sprinkle of chopped fresh parsley and a dollop of sour cream, if desired. And that's it!
