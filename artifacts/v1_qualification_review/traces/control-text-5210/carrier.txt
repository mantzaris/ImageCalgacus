 A home cook begins by selecting a variety of vegetables, such as carrots, celery, and potatoes, that are in season and of good quality. With a sharp knife, they chop the vegetables into small, uniform pieces so that they cook evenly. Next, the cook sautés the chopped vegetables in a small amount of olive oil in a large pot, this helps to bring out their natural flavors and tenderize them. After the vegetables are lightly browned, the cook adds a can of diced tomatoes and a quart of vegetable broth to the pot, stirring everything together to combine the flavors. The cook then adds a sprinkle of dried thyme and a pinch of salt to the pot, the thyme adds a slightly earthy flavor and the salt enhances the natural flavors of the vegetables. As the soup heats up and the flavors meld together, the cook is able to taste and adjust the seasoning as needed, adding more salt, pepper, or herbs if desired. Finally, the cook removes the pot from the heat and lets the soup cool slightly before serving, this helps to prevent scalding and allows the flavors to settle. A home cook prepares a simple vegetable soup by using a variety of techniques including chopping, sautéing, and seasoning, and by allowing the flavors to meld together. The resulting soup is a delicious and satisfying meal that is easy to make and perfect for a weeknight dinner.
