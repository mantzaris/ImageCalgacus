How to Write a Simple Vegetable Soup Recipe – A Step-by-Step Guide
If you are looking for a delicious and easy-to-make vegetable soup recipe, you have come to the right place. This guide will walk you through the steps you need to follow to prepare a simple vegetable soup using a continuous prose style.

Step 1: Choose the vegetables and seasonings
Choose a variety of colorful vegetables such as cabbage, carrots, celery, and onions, which are chopped fresh and thrown into a large pot. You can also add some aromatics like garlic, bay leaves, and thyme to give it a richer flavor.

Step 2: Prepare the vegetables for cooking
Chop the selected vegetables and seasonings into smaller pieces, so they can cook evenly and quickly. Make sure you remove any seeds, skins, or stems as they can become tough or bitter during cooking.

Step 3: Soften the vegetables with oil and water
Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat, then add the chopped vegetables, garlic, and aromatics. Cook for about 5-7 minutes, until the vegetables are softened and fragrant. Then, add the vegetable broth, water, and tomatoes, and check for seasoning.

Step 4: Add texture and body to the soup
Add any additional ingredients, such as pasta, rice, or beans, to give the soup texture and body. Cook for another 10-15 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the soup has thickened.

Step 5: Season and adjust the soup
Taste the soup and adjust the seasoning as needed. Add salt, pepper, or other spices to balance the flavor.

Step 6: Serve and enjoy
Ladle the soup into bowls and serve with a sprinkle of fresh herbs, such as parsley or cilantro, or a dollop of crème fraîche. Serve hot, but not too hot, as it can cause discomfort to the palate. Allow the soup to cool slightly before serving, and then enjoy the delicious and satisfying flavors.
