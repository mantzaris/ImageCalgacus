 As you begin to prepare a simple vegetable soup, you start by gathering the necessary ingredients from your pantry and fridge. You select a variety of colorful vegetables such as bell peppers, carrots, zucchini, and potatoes, and chop them into bite-sized pieces. You also add some aromatic herbs like thyme and rosemary to give your soup a savory flavor.
Next, you heat some oil in a large pot over medium heat, and sauté the chopped vegetables until they are tender and lightly browned. You then add a can of diced tomatoes and a cup of vegetable broth to the pot, stirring well to combine.
After that, you bring the mixture to a boil, reducing the heat to low and allowing it to simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the flavors have melded together and the vegetables are tender. During this time, you can also season the soup with salt, pepper, and any other herbs or spices you like. Once the soup is ready, you can serve it hot, garnished with a sprinkle of parsley or chives, and perhaps some crusty bread on the side.
