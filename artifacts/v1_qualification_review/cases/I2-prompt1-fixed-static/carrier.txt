**Sunday Walk Along a Quiet Creek*

In this still air of end-Aug ust afternon a warm hiver sun, where sunray beams creep across moss-crisp ground & tree bocks' morned fur & branches like outstreck hands of ancient sentriuls reach across this land... & this soft earth. Where creek-waves glide with steady, unobstructive glibble-gliding of slowest waterway to this forest' s verd' & brown's quiet center, we're not hurry; no rusted pace & quiet step's a soft sym. We wander here.

Saplings with green bark glow; new life springs forth anew with morning dew's refreshing gift in suns warmth where it is home... A gentle broom & leaf-ridden brooms sway in harmony like an endless chant's soft undertows; as breeze, too soft a lover for leaf's delicate whispers - no gust that'll scatter the still & soft dance in breeze - whispers & carries. Sunbeam filters to creek & the shadows where shadows grow; shadows in moss that crevases. Moss, that sough-tingly sound of soft silence - that whispers the creek itself into still, slow waves to our path, to my boots' sound.

In this temper-mental climate the air's scent & sounds consens, the air is of this & for it all & it is, & this too it & the world, as much to & to all we pass here on quiet day in & quiet time of the day as to this. & of me in me here. And we wander, slow. No one's footstpe in distance or speed & pace nor none that rush or force - & yet in every sound. & quiet is the voice.

The scent rises up - the air. We are still there.
