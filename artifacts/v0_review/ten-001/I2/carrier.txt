In some respects my mind is elsewhere when we walk down forest lanes today because the leaves under each gentle footprint still rustling on some invisible wind whispers to another day, a time past when our worries and troubles seemed farther in perspective as farmland stretches in an almost unspoils horizon and a distant creek we'll follow into deeper, shaded depths and I let a branch sway across, and its weight is so inscruti that its release becomes an imperfett moment of freedom the only way. Yet this world around and inside still exists. Leaves and pine and oak they are so untrampled and untouched their shapes as well-defined against that of a human figure on that background I see as if they would survive any chaos to return like some kind in time, while I in my mind keep trying the shapeless outlines and unresistant edges a fleeting life, an imperilment that can not hold still or even be. Still. A tree grows up around itself its limbs unfolding the very earth to reach for more space more. The world has time; only we must take our steps and the forest offers. A path is never set in this, it has a fluid, shifting center where every stone on ground I can feel I know but where will the wind lead I am walking, the creek ahead, its gullies the silence I can't help think this walk as it we move into deeper parts I know to a time. Then it would. Be there to wait for all else but it can. It doesn.
