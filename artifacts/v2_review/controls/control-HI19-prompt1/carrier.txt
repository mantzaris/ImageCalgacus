 

The air today is crisp and cool, carrying the scent of damp earth and decaying leaves. As I walk, the rustling of leaves beneath my feet serves as a gentle accompaniment, a soothing background hum that calms the mind. The canopy above is a vibrant tapestry of greens, with sunlight filtering through in dappled patterns, illuminating the forest floor in a warm, inviting glow. The trees, towering and majestic, stretch upwards, their branches swaying gently in the breeze. A small clearing opens up before me, and I pause to take in the serene beauty of this temperate sanctuary. In this moment, all worries and concerns seem to fade away, leaving only the simple joys of being present in this natural world. The gentle rustle of leaves, the soft call of a distant bird, and the sweet scent of blooming wildflowers all combine to create a sense of peace and tranquility, a balm to the soul. As I continue on my walk, the forest closes in around me once more, enveloping me in its calming embrace. I breathe deeply, feeling the tension and stress melt away, replaced by a sense of connection to this land and all its wonders. In this peaceful forest, I am free to be, to simply exist, and to let the natural world work its magic upon my weary soul. The world may be chaotic and unpredictable, but in this moment, peace and serenity reign supreme. And for now, that is enough.  Copyright 2015 Marlene Harris-Taylor. All rights reserved.
