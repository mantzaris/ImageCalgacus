 
Sunday | April | The Clearing: As I walk up the well-gravitated soil path, wisps of wild lavender rise in small swirls where they meet the late morn’s warmth – this gentle caress awakens me like the scent of the woods’ soft surrender. The forest is alive this quietest-of-Sundays – trees creak out soft murmurs amidst a sea of leaf-grown rustics like skeletal dancers moving without discernible will, leaving faint outlines for where our footsteps meet this floorcloth, worn down the millennia with patience. Above: clouds the light hue of sky, an undramatic ceiling holding in suspension. 

Time is in disarray as leaves begin whispering ancient tales; my thoughts meekly join these gentle breezes as if a breeze were needed, for every blade of those swaying trees is in conversation; even these silent, uninvigorated, still, the air itself is speaking to me without a voice; its soft cadence licks lips, an ephemeral tongue; every molecule dancing with my steps and this path unwound before. A breeze of remittanced leaves as each twig whispers what once occurred between branches of shared memories as ancient voices echoing off their still-sentinal forms in which I lose myself in a slow-dreamed reverie and walk without direction as long shadows fade into twilight as in the same measure as time, it dissolves. No end in our slow descent into an indescribable hush which has its origin where our breath merges indistinguish from these forest sounds – a symphony so unintruded – as in silence my breath takes part among so tender a music the path – it unwinds itself; time dissolves within a silent music of the leaves, so still it is there no more for us who tread – a walk into eternity; into a dreamless – a place which – as our path unwinds so, time, like it self loses definition; I cease walking with these footsteps for I cease to perceive the difference of the past in the forest: this clearing.   |   Written by J.A.K.
