A soft filtering begins through me now: thoughts sharpen by association as fingers weave into leaves to feel the edges of memory - an unbroken string linking my past steps to a path worn from beneath ancient branches overhead as their leaf-lings release tiny parachuted packets into morning, carrying whispered conversations through an unseen breeze, whispers only discernble between the gentle hushing wind-springs where water once played.

My hand finds smooth peaches beneath an open eye-shaped canopy now where shadows settle like an inanimate map to be read only if you take one measured stroll through it. My gaze, tracing fingers to their natural place on a trunk thickly cloven and etted against seasons, finds an uninvadibility there that calks an inner silence.

A bird in nearby distance offers no surprise yet it remains as though this very moment is not meant. Time, if it holds an order - or is merely its opposite's opposite then there's not another thing but to continue in this gentle cadenza - to listen, observe, the trees and the earth in concert for no other song save to accompany this quiet - for if they had to speak at least one part - for I sense there will only emerge this sym- the -phony that only these, these quietest ones can compose: for here all has its appointed, all in due - due order. This - the moment I was looking - or so I feel I may not know until it begins again like it never ceased to exist as if only the memory remained. A breeze moves in the trees. The leaves begin whisper - in harmony to their song, they now - speak.
