  
April, late in the dry spring day. Soft sunlight filtering through lances of leaf-gem green, each a hundred times more green than any memory of summer sunshine in August has right to lay claim: such clarity! Here the trees rise as giants – cleft trunks standing watch while canopy leaves shimmer a thousand points – a verdant mist that wraps round this moment like warm breath around cupped palm leaves of ferny softness.
Every foot-fall a tiny earthquake stuns a world that sleeps. Earthworm trails and roots writhe free from crumbling earth. Leaves creak as a slow symphony unfolds from the heart-pulsed thronged orchestra within, releasing an ancient perfume, rich as wet stones worn into submission by ancient waters; ancient voices whispered across the years from these forest lips: I've always said there exists another, more precise dimension – I believe, this one: a hidden dimension in time-space wherein time's river runs back into time-forests such as, say, right beside the old canted stone here where some poor old settler rested on their final way out; some long-lost love, their story lost, their secrets silenced as earth swallows stone...  
The way this walk unfurls through this still and verdant, where every sound – and the rust in the silence! Each creaking joint like worn wooden shoes – tells more story to our feet while each blade stabs a shard of silence back like an open wound in our skin...
Here's our chance encounter - in some hollow a small doe and the wretched foal, its spindly fore-antlene, as it stumbles; it'll make do in due balance for some later moment - while I pause - in an ancient still life where life is already a part. Timelessness searing its imprint. Time is what they called "what we know we know". This, my time. In silence's depths this – my breath; in each still-life – my own story...
A rustling! Leaves' chorus swiveling round this axis: the wayward rabbit – the forest is a place in a dream, the dreamscape within - a story that, I am but just the protagonist in; this I believe – that my walk today will unfold another scene... or, maybe this - that in all that I touch I will unfold; that is what my footsteps will leave...
As dusk, now a hushed veil falls: a thin line on which each blade stands: our footpath - an edge – I cross, into – and now we're part: a tale told to trees who already hold so – many tales, their tales woven from these very moments – threads I – I now part. This: this very walk now part. I am not the story – story has – has its hands. As dusk now settles the story in.
